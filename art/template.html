Guide character — drawing template

100 × 112 canvas. Print at 100%, or open in a drawing app and trace on a layer above.

The one rule that must be exact: the feet sit on the baseline and the head fits the head guide, on every pose. Registration is what makes a pose swap look intentional instead of jumpy.
  • Baseline (solid): feet touch here. Nothing below it.
  • Head guide (dashed circle): roughly where the head sits — you can break it, but keep it consistent across poses.
  • Centre line: the body's axis. A pose can lean off it; the feet shouldn't wander far.
  • Bleed edge: keep everything inside the box. An arm that crosses it gets clipped in the app.
Draw in this order: attentive first (used on 7 screens), then talk, then think. Those three cover 16 of the 20 uses. Point and cheer appear once each — you can map them onto talk and wave later and draw 4 poses instead of 6.

Keep: non-human creature · distinct silhouette a 5-year-old can tell apart · never sad, scared or hurt — calm and attentive is the worst it gets · no red anywhere, red is reserved for pain intensity.
