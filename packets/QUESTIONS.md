# Every question, and what it rests on

Generated by `scripts/questions-report.mjs` — do not hand-edit.

> **Before citing this anywhere, read `packets/LIMITATIONS.md`.** Nothing here
> has been clinically reviewed. The citations show what a question was derived
> from; they do not mean a clinician checked the derivation, the wording, or
> what was left out.

`EVIDENCE.md` is the bibliography — which sources each packet consulted. This
is the other direction: start from a question a child is actually asked and
find what it rests on.

## How to read this

**110 questions currently reach children; 75 sit below the cut.**
Across 19 packets.

A child answers **5 questions under age 8 and 6 from age 8 up**, spent
round-robin across every body area they tapped, plus a reserved "has this
happened to you before". So only the top of each packet is ever reached.
Numbered entries ship; entries marked — are kept, cited and ranked but not
currently asked. They become reachable if the cap grows, or when a duration
or age filter removes something above them.

**Establishes** is the fact a question pins down. Facts are shared across
packets deliberately, and only one question per fact is ever asked — so a
child who taps two body areas is not asked the same thing twice.
