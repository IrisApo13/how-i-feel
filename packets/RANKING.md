form** — the rankings below are already applied. Regenerate it after any
`itemRank` change, or delete it; a stale copy that contradicts the metas is
worse than no copy.

The 14 pre-Wave-C rankings were confirmed by iris on 2026-09-08, item by item.
**The five Wave C packets have not had that pass** — their orderings are still
agent-proposed and still carry *"Proposed, not yet confirmed by review."*
Previous values are backed up in `packets/.itemrank-backup-2026-09-08.json`.

**The cap is what makes this matter:** 5 questions under age 8 and 6 from 8 up,
spent round-robin across every group the child tapped, plus a reserved "has this
happened before". Only the top of each list is ever reached. Items below the line
stay accepted and cited — they ship if the cap grows, or when a duration or age
filter removes something above them.

- **[flag]** — red-flag tag. JUDGEMENT, NOT A CITATION. Wave C packets are untagged.
- **n src** — sources the packet cites for that item.
- **shared** — another packet owns the same fact and may claim the slot.
